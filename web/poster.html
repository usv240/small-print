A poster for the reading-glasses rack

Most ready-made reading glasses are sold from a rack: in pharmacies, markets, supermarkets and clinics. The rack is exactly where people guess their strength. This free A4 poster puts a two-minute test right there.

  1. Choose the language for the poster (the test itself lets each person choose too).
  2. Print it on A4 or US Letter, in colour or black and white.
  3. Put it next to the rack. Customers scan the code, take the test, and the result tells them which two strengths to try first, then checks the pair they pick.

The QR code links to the test. No sign-up, nothing to install, and no camera images leave the phone. Results are counted anonymously on the impact page. It helps people choose non-prescription reading glasses and is not an eye exam.
